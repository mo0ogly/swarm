## 4. What worked

- **Retained evidence at every failure**: the three UI races each have their
  dated directory and a DIAGNOSIS.md; diagnosis could be carried out outside
  execution (CDP on a live page) and the fix aimed at the exact cause without
  touching the product.
- **Waiting on a terminal effect rather than an intermediate state**: the
  Escape race was lifted by waiting for the text set after the post-apply
  re-read, not a satisfiable disabled-button state.
- **Strict recipe contract**: the immediate refusal of the unplanned field
  (`inputs_note`) avoided an ambiguous recipe; the note lives in the README.
- **Scope division**: no engine code modified while `confidentiality.rs`
  carried the concurrent BF-14a diff; the BLP-06 need was transmitted as a
  verifiable contract (named red→green criteria).
- **Independent re-verify before RETEX**: `recipe.py verify` re-reads the
  retained bytes without re-execution — an honest basis for citing a
  historical PASS.

## 5. Most costly failure mechanisms (observed)

1. **UI wait satisfiable in flight** (~2 iterations): intermediate states
   (button disabled by `guarded()`, earlier status text) satisfy a
   `waitForFunction` during the request; Escape then closed the dialog and
   focus restoration targeted a still-disabled button. General fix: wait only
   for effects set after the internal re-read. Verifiable by construction for
   future journeys.
2. **Silent controls outside the modal dialog** (1 iteration): controls inside
   a closed `<details>` accept the click with no effect; prior opening
   required — already known from the 31 Oct RETEX, reappeared on a new
   surface.
3. **One single object for eight journeys** (1 iteration): the producer
   refuses a second recording of the same triple (exact-once, expected) — the
   driver had to create a distinct object per FR/EN×theme×width combination.
   Test design, not a product defect.
4. **Incomplete driver contract** (`public_output`, flag scope): two
   iterations lost on defects of the test harness itself; the established
   precedent (`blp_quarantine_ui.cjs`) had not been consulted before writing.
5. **Traceability drift** (`version_history` without a BLP-03a entry): found
   at RETEX time, not at delivery; the "documentation in the same increment"
   rule held for the plans but not for the version register. Fix applied
   (+4-line diff).
