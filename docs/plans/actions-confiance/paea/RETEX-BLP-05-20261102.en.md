## 4. What worked

- Empirical probes before assertions: the revision semantics (`current()`
  requires the entry carrying the current trust revision; `current_revision`
  reports the pre-apply value) were established by probing the binary, not
  deduced — no wrong assertion reached the end of the journey.
- The established `confidentiality.rs` pattern (TMPDIR required, `/tmp`
  refusal, birth record, conservative `Drop`) reused as-is for the
  `preparation_calls.rs` fix: a single iteration.
- Refusing the rebuild in the presence of concurrent WIP and reusing the
  already-prepared candidate avoided compiling someone else's work.

## 5. Most costly failure mechanisms (observed)

1. **Policy-renewal semantics not anticipated** (~4 journey iterations):
   after any trust-revision change, the old policy entry becomes unusable for
   `current()` until an explicit renewal; the refusal surfaces upstream
   (`blp-policy-stale`) before classification is even evaluated. Cost:
   journey iterations instead of an upfront probe — fixed by probing, then
   inserting the third `blp-policy-apply`.
2. **Anti-declassification guard scans the entire history**: a public
   rebinding of a digest once bound secret refuses forever; the no-write-down
   test had to be restructured around a second connection (fresh digest)
   rather than a public rebinding of the secret-bound digest. Detected in
   execution, not in design.
3. **Request helpers with implicit fields**: `TypeError: dict() got multiple
   values for keyword argument` — overridable fields made explicit named
   parameters; AST check ruled out real duplicates.
4. **Producer `/tmp` leak** (inherited, not this session): `preparation_calls.rs`
   depended on the default `TMPDIR`; discovered during the cleanliness check,
   fixed on the established pattern, regression guard = the test itself now
   fails outside the fixture area.
5. **Auto-mode classifier unavailable** (repeated refusals, including on
   `rm`): read-only waiting, then handing the `rm` action to the user, who
   executed it; no blind retry of a refused action.
